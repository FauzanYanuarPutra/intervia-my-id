# Product Principles

Status: repo audit 2026-07-11.

## What Lajukan Is

Lajukan is a platform for Indonesian business needs. It combines search/discovery, local business profiles, listings, community/reels distribution, chat/WhatsApp contact, support/ops, and transaction primitives.

## Product Rules

1. Search is the main discovery spine.
2. Listing, store profile, public profile, chat, WhatsApp, and map must reinforce each other.
3. "Mencari" and "Menawarkan" are market activities, not separate isolated products.
4. Community and reels are engagement/distribution layers, not transaction categories.
5. WhatsApp is a first-class contact behavior, but internal chat remains important for history, privacy, support, and measurement.
6. AI helps reduce work, rank/summarize, and suggest next steps; it must not invent business facts.
7. Trust signals must be tied to verifiable source data.
8. Location UX must distinguish city/address from precise coordinate distance.
9. Do not overclaim payment, escrow, refund, or verification maturity without end-to-end evidence.
10. Prefer one canonical route/flow per user need.
11. Start with dense regional clusters before chasing national breadth.
12. Rank business needs by total serviceability, not only by cheapest price.

## Primary User Goals

- Find business needs quickly.
- Compare suppliers, services, tools, places, and local businesses.
- Contact providers through chat or WhatsApp.
- Publish a need or offer without complex forms.
- Build trust through profile, verification, media, reviews, and reports.

## Product Guardrail

Any new feature should answer: does this help a user move from need -> relevant result -> trusted profile/listing -> conversation/contact -> action?

For cluster work, also ask: does this improve density, trust, response, or demand visibility in the launch region?
