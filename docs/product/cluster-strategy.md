# Cluster Strategy

Status: product strategy input 2026-07-11. Market-size statements in source discussions are treated as hypotheses until validated with sourced research and Lajukan analytics.

## Core Positioning

Lajukan should not start as a generic national marketplace or a full super-app. The strongest near-term positioning is:

> Lajukan is a regional business-needs network for Indonesia. It helps people find what they need to start, run, and grow a business.

Short product line:

> Lajukan - jaringan kebutuhan dan peluang usaha Indonesia.

The product should move users from:

1. Need.
2. Relevant local or serviceable providers.
3. Trusted profile/listing.
4. WhatsApp or chat contact.
5. Follow-up, quote, order, or learning signal.

## Strategic Bet

The most realistic impact is not "reforming Indonesia" through one large marketplace. The realistic impact is improving information flow and business networks:

| Current Problem | Desired Lajukan Shift |
| --- | --- |
| Supplier information is scattered | Suppliers can be searched and compared |
| Local businesses are invisible online | Businesses have profiles, catalogs, location, and contact paths |
| Demand is hidden in private chats/groups | Needs become structured demand data |
| Trust depends only on personal referral | Trust is supported by verification, reports, reviews, and visitability |
| Regions operate separately | Regions become visible business clusters |
| Buyers compare only price | Buyers compare total fit: price, location, shipping, technician, warranty, response, trust |

## Why Cluster First

Indonesia's geography makes a pure national-lowest-price marketplace a poor starting point for many business needs. A freezer, machine, booth, ruko, technician, or service provider is not only a product price problem. It is also a distance, delivery, installation, warranty, and after-sales problem.

The product should therefore prioritize dense regional clusters before national scale.

Cluster-first means:

- Select one launch region.
- Build enough supply and demand density there.
- Measure search, contact, and response quality.
- Recruit missing supplier categories based on actual zero-result and demand data.
- Expand only when one regional playbook works.

Candidate first cluster:

- Bandung Raya, because the product discussions and current examples already use Bandung/Cimahi often, and it has many natural business clusters such as culinary, packaging, convection, creative services, and equipment needs.

This is a candidate, not a final decision. Final launch region should use data from supplier availability, user interviews, search demand, ops reach, and listing quality.

## Product Model

### 1. Two Market Sides

Lajukan must support both sides equally:

- `Mencari`: "Saya membutuhkan mesin, bahan, jasa, tempat usaha, atau peluang."
- `Menawarkan`: "Saya menyediakan mesin, bahan, jasa, tempat usaha, atau peluang."

When supply is thin, `Mencari` prevents the product from feeling empty. Each need captures:

- requested item/service;
- category and subcategory;
- city, district, radius, or service area;
- budget range;
- timing;
- quantity;
- contact preference;
- matching outcome.

These needs become the data source for supplier acquisition and ranking improvements.

### 2. Location As A Platform Capability

`Usaha Sekitar` should not behave only as an isolated category. Location should apply across the platform:

- Mesin terdekat.
- Bahan terdekat.
- Jasa terdekat.
- Tempat usaha terdekat.
- Supplier terdekat.
- Komunitas terdekat.
- Teknisi atau layanan purna jual terdekat.

Ranking should not be cheapest-first. A better early ranking formula is:

```text
intent relevance
+ location/service-area fit
+ trust and verification
+ response quality
+ product/listing completeness
+ delivery/install/warranty availability
+ price reasonableness
+ recent demand and engagement
```

Distance must only be shown when safe coordinate data exists. If only city or address text exists, show textual location instead of precise meters/kilometers.

### 3. WhatsApp As Primary Contact, Still Measured

For early Indonesia UMKM adoption, WhatsApp should be a first-class CTA. Do not force all users into internal chat before the habit exists.

Lajukan should add structure around WhatsApp:

- generated opening message;
- listing identity and lead code;
- buyer location/need summary when consented;
- safety warning;
- click tracking;
- seller response tracking where possible;
- follow-up task or CRM connection event for high-intent actions only.

Internal chat remains important for history, support, privacy, and later CRM workflows.

### 4. Assisted Onboarding

Many good suppliers do not have clean digital catalogs. Lajukan should reduce seller work through:

- photo upload assisted by AI when fast and conservative;
- manual/admin-assisted profile creation;
- WhatsApp catalog import later;
- auto category/tag/description suggestions;
- location picking by map;
- simple "hubungi saya untuk melengkapi postingan" option;
- human review for high-risk categories.

AI must not invent product facts. Suggestions need user confirmation and correction.

### 5. Local Trust

Trust should be concrete and local, not only a decorative badge.

Useful trust levels:

- phone verified;
- WhatsApp active;
- address checked;
- can be visited;
- identity checked;
- business document checked;
- distributor/association/community verified;
- response quality;
- no active serious reports;
- transaction/lead history when available.

For machines, tools, places, and franchise/opportunity categories, visitability, documentation, warranty, and report history matter more than a generic "verified" badge.

### 6. Do Not Build Logistics First

Lajukan should not become a logistics company in the early stage. It should help users understand logistics/serviceability:

- seller supports delivery or pickup;
- supported cargo/expedition;
- estimated shipping range if available;
- installation requirement;
- technician coverage;
- warranty or after-sales support;
- whether direct survey/COD is possible.

## Category Strategy

Do not delete the broader vision, but do not treat every module as equal in the MVP.

### Core Transaction Categories

1. Mesin & Alat.
2. Bahan Usaha.
3. Jasa.
4. Tempat Usaha.

### Platform Capability

- Usaha Sekitar: location mode across the core categories, not only a separate destination.

### Growth And Learning Layers

- Peluang Usaha.
- Komunitas.
- Video/Reels.

These should feed the core marketplace:

```text
Video inspires
-> Peluang explains business model
-> Lajukan finds machines, materials, services, and places
-> Community helps users learn and build network
```

## Staged Roadmap

### 0-3 Months: Regional Needs Map

Recommended focus:

- one region;
- three categories: Mesin & Alat, Bahan Usaha, Jasa;
- search;
- needs/request posting;
- listings;
- business profiles;
- location;
- WhatsApp CTA;
- basic verification;
- analytics.

Target indicators:

- 100 reachable suppliers;
- 1,000 quality listings or structured offers;
- search results are not empty for priority queries;
- WhatsApp contacts receive responses;
- repeated needs are visible by category and city.

### 3-6 Months: Business Cluster Playbooks

Pick three business types and build package-like discovery:

- culinary and drinks;
- laundry;
- toko/warung.

For each business type, connect the full need chain: machines, materials, packaging, technicians, services, place, and relevant opportunities.

### 6-12 Months: Matching Engine

Use AI and search intelligence to:

- understand everyday Indonesian queries;
- match needs to suppliers;
- group similar listings;
- fix typos and synonyms;
- summarize comparisons;
- identify unmet demand;
- flag suspicious listings;
- recommend supplier recruitment targets.

The model must retrieve real Lajukan data first. AI explains and ranks; it should not hallucinate suppliers.

### After Regional Proof: Cluster Network

When multiple regions are dense, Lajukan can show:

- which region produces what;
- where demand is underserved;
- which suppliers can serve other cities;
- where distributor opportunities exist;
- which categories need local recruitment.

This is when Lajukan starts becoming a map of Indonesia's business ecosystem.

## Analytics Required

Cluster strategy needs these metrics from day one:

- search query;
- city/district/radius;
- zero-result query;
- listing impression/click;
- WhatsApp click;
- chat start;
- need/request created;
- supplier response;
- quote/request match;
- report/safety issue;
- category and subcategory;
- distance bucket;
- service-area fit;
- seller verification tier;
- completion/correction rate for AI-assisted create.

Dashboards should answer:

- What do people need in this region?
- Which searches have no good supply?
- Which suppliers get contacted but do not respond?
- Which categories have supply but no demand?
- Which local cluster should be expanded next?

## Decisions Implied By This Strategy

- Start dense and regional before broad national expansion.
- Treat `Mencari` demand data as a first-class product asset.
- Make location/serviceability part of ranking, not just a filter.
- Use WhatsApp as primary early CTA while keeping measurement and safety.
- Keep community/video as acquisition and education layers, not the main transaction category.
- Delay heavy logistics, payments, and fully automated AI actions until core search/contact trust is proven.

## Hypotheses To Validate

| Hypothesis | Validation Method |
| --- | --- |
| Bandung Raya is the best first cluster | Supplier audit, interviews, search demand, ops reach |
| Three categories are enough for early density | Search/contact conversion and zero-result rate |
| Buyers prefer serviceable nearby providers over cheapest remote providers for machines/tools | Search A/B, interviews, contact conversion |
| `Mencari` posts improve cold-start supply acquisition | Supplier response rate and matched connection rate |
| WhatsApp primary CTA increases contact conversion | WhatsApp click/contact rate vs internal chat |
| Assisted onboarding improves listing quality | Completion time, accepted AI fields, admin correction rate |
